# Boiler Feed Pump Low Flow Procedure

Document ID: PROC-BFP-001
Document Type: Operating Procedure
Revision: 3

## Immediate operator actions

When a boiler feed pump low-flow alarm is active:

1. Confirm the alarm using the local flow indication.
2. Verify pump suction pressure.
3. Verify discharge pressure.
4. Check suction and discharge valve positions.
5. Check the suction strainer differential pressure.
6. Transfer to the standby pump if approved operating conditions cannot be maintained.
7. Notify the control room supervisor.

## Safety

Do not bypass pump protection interlocks. Do not open pressurized equipment. Follow site isolation and permit-to-work requirements.

## Escalation

Escalate to the reliability engineer when the alarm recurs more than five times in a shift or when abnormal vibration, noise, or temperature is observed.
