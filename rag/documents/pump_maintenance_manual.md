## Low flow troubleshooting

Recurring low flow can be caused by:

- Blocked suction strainer
- Partially closed suction valve
- Cavitation
- Impeller wear
- Incorrect transmitter calibration
- Low suction pressure

Inspect the suction strainer differential pressure and pump vibration trend before scheduling intrusive maintenance.
